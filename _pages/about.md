I am a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://www.ust.hk/), advised by [Professor Junxian He](https://www.ece.ust.hk/~junhe/) in the [HKUST NLP Group](https://www.ece.ust.hk/hongchi-junhe/). I previously graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://english.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024, where I completed my bachelor's degree (B.Eng., 2020-2024).

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with research interests in:

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
